### 1. Griefing is not allowed
Griefing will result in a ban from the server. We highly recommend you claim your land anyway to stay safe.

### 2. No lavacasts or giant atrocities
This is only allowed if the lavacast is part of actual build construction.

### 3. Do not maliciously claim land
Do not claim land simply to prevent others from building or claiming in an area. This is a scummy tactic and those claims will be removed. You can claim around your main base claim to a certain extent.

### 4. Claim Expiration
Claims get erased after 90 days of inactivity. All you need to do to prevent this is log in. Players with 80,000 total claim blocks or more are exempt from this.
