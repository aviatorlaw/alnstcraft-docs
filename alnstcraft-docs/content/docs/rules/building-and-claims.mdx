### 1. Griefing is not allowed
Griefing will result in a ban from the server[cite: 3]. We highly recommend you claim your land anyway to stay safe[cite: 3].

### 2. No lavacasts or giant atrocities
This is only allowed if the lavacast is part of actual build construction[cite: 3].

### 3. Do not maliciously claim land
Do not claim land simply to prevent others from building or claiming in an area[cite: 3]. This is a scummy tactic and those claims will be removed[cite: 3]. You can claim around your main base claim to a certain extent[cite: 3].

### 4. Claim Expiration
Claims get erased after 90 days of inactivity[cite: 3]. All you need to do to prevent this is log in[cite: 3]. Players with 80,000 total claim blocks or more are exempt from this[cite: 3].
