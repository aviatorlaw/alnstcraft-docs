## Homes
Homes are waypoints you can set to teleport back to at any time[cite: 3]. By default, everyone has at least one home they can set, plus their linked bed[cite: 3]. Supporters with designated roles have two homes they can set[cite: 3].

<Callout type="warn">
  We recommend that you don't rely on your bed to teleport back to your base. You could accidentally set your respawn point to another bed and lose your base completely[cite: 3].
</Callout>

* `/sethome (name)` - Create a home[cite: 3].
* `/home (name)` - Teleport to your home[cite: 3].
* `/delhome (name)` - Delete a home[cite: 3].

## Warps
Warps give players easier access to towns, shops, and farms[cite: 3]. Warps cost **$10,000** each to create, with no upper limit[cite: 3]. 

You can access the warp browser by typing `/pw`[cite: 3]. 

### Managing your Warp
There are 11 options for managing a warp you own[cite: 3]:

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| **Teleportation** | Set a price for using your warp[cite: 3]. |
| **Change Type** | Change the category it belongs to[cite: 3]. |
| **Change Name** | Change the display name or the warp name itself[cite: 3]. |
| **Change Icon/Desc** | Change the item icon or description text[cite: 3]. |
| **Privacy** | Change access (Open, Closed, Password)[cite: 3]. |
| **Feature Warp** | Add warp to the featured section for 8 hours for $5,000[cite: 3]. |
| **Relocate** | Change the warp location to your current position[cite: 3]. |
| **Transfer** | Give ownership to another user. *Warning: You lose all management access and staff may not help you reverse this[cite: 3].* |
| **Remove** | Deletes your warp. *Warning: Irreversible, and you will only be refunded $2,000[cite: 3].* |
