## Homes
Homes are waypoints you can set to teleport back to at any time. By default, everyone has at least one home they can set, plus their linked bed. Supporters with designated roles have two homes they can set.

<Callout type="warn">
  We recommend that you don't rely on your bed to teleport back to your base. You could accidentally set your respawn point to another bed and lose your base completely.
</Callout>

* `/sethome (name)` - Create a home.
* `/home (name)` - Teleport to your home.
* `/delhome (name)` - Delete a home.

## Warps
Warps give players easier access to towns, shops, and farms. Warps cost **$10,000** each to create, with no upper limit. 

You can access the warp browser by typing `/pw`. 

### Managing your Warp
There are 11 options for managing a warp you own:

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| **Teleportation** | Set a price for using your warp. |
| **Change Type** | Change the category it belongs to. |
| **Change Name** | Change the display name or the warp name itself. |
| **Change Icon/Desc** | Change the item icon or description text. |
| **Privacy** | Change access (Open, Closed, Password). |
| **Feature Warp** | Add warp to the featured section for 8 hours for $5,000. |
| **Transfer** | Give ownership to another user. *Warning: You lose all management access and staff may not help you reverse this.* |
| **Remove** | Deletes your warp. *Warning: Irreversible, and you will only be refunded $2,000.* |
