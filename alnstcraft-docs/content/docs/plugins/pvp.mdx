---
title: PVP (ZPvPToggle)
icon: Swords
---

PVP is managed by **ZPvPToggle**. 

By default, PVP is toggled **off** for players in all dimensions. You can switch your status by typing `/pvp on` or `/pvp off`. Players with PVP enabled have a red circle around their feet.

*KeepInventory is disabled on ALNSTCRAFT, as decided by poll.*
