We use **GriefPrevention** to allow the creation of claims[cite: 3]. Claims are areas of land that you own to prevent griefing, theft, and manage who can build there[cite: 3].

### How to Claim Land
Claims are managed with a **golden shovel**[cite: 3].

1. Hold a golden shovel[cite: 3].
2. Right-click one corner of the area you want to claim[cite: 3].
3. Go to the opposite corner and right-click it with the shovel[cite: 3].

You should be told your claim was created[cite: 3]. If it fails, you may not have enough claim blocks (earned by being in-game) or it overlaps another claim[cite: 3].

### Useful Tools & Commands
* **Inspect Claims:** Right-click the ground with a **stick** to check if there is a claim and who owns it[cite: 3].
* **Delete Claim:** Use `/abandonclaim` while standing inside it[cite: 3].
