PVP is managed by **ZPvPToggle**[cite: 3]. 

By default, PVP is toggled **off** for players in all dimensions[cite: 3]. You can switch your status by typing `/pvp on` or `/pvp off`[cite: 3]. Players with PVP enabled have a red circle around their feet[cite: 3].

*KeepInventory is disabled on ALNSTCRAFT, as decided by poll[cite: 3].*
