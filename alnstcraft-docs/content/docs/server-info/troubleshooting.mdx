If you are having trouble joining ALNSTCRAFT or linking your account, check out the answers below before opening a ticket.

## DiscordSRV & Account Linking Issues

### I can't send the code to the bot.
If you DM the bot the code given to you and are hit with "Your message could not be delivered", your Discord privacy settings could be blocking direct messages from server members.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    #### Open Discord User Settings
    Click the **Gear icon** next to your profile in Discord.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    #### Navigate to Privacy & Safety
    Go to **Privacy & Safety** in the left sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    #### Enable Direct Messages
    Toggle **"Allow direct messages from server members"** to **ON** for `Alien Stage`.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    #### Retry Linking
    Try send the code to the bot again before rejoining.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connection & Access Issues

### I should have access to the server, yet it doesn't let me in.
If your account is linked but you are still not allowed to join the Minecraft server, your Discord account may be missing a required role.

To access ALNSTCRAFT, your Discord account must hold **at least one** of the following roles:
* `@VIVIs`
* `@MC Access`
* `@MC Tester`
* `@Moderators`

<Callout type="warn">
  If you have one of the roles above and are still being kicked, open a ticket.
</Callout>

### I haven't gottem my Booster rank yet?
If you boosted the Discord server but your permissions or perks have not updated in-game, LuckPerms may not be working properly with DiscordSRV.

* **Re-log**: Disconnect and rejoin the server to force a permission update.
* **Wait for Sync**: DiscordSRV usually resyncs every few minutes for all online players. If your rank is still missing after 15 minutes, open a ticket.
