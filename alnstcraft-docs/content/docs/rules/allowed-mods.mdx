---
title: Allowed Mods & Clients
icon: wrench
---

If you are unsure whether a client or mod is allowed, open a ticket[cite: 3].

* **General Clients:** Optifine, Lunar, Badlion, Xaero's Map, SimpleVoiceChat, PlasmoVoice, Voxy, DistantHorizons, etc. are allowed as long as they don't cause an unfair advantage[cite: 3].
* **Litematica:** Easy Place is allowed, but printing and mid-air placing are **not** allowed[cite: 3].
* **Lighting:** Fullbright / Gamma modifications are allowed[cite: 3].
