---
title: Allowed Mods & Clients
icon: Wrench
---

If you are unsure whether a client or mod is allowed, open a ticket.

* **General Clients:** Optifine, Lunar, Badlion, Xaero's Map, SimpleVoiceChat, PlasmoVoice, Voxy, DistantHorizons, etc. are allowed as long as they don't cause an unfair advantage.
* **Litematica:** Easy Place is allowed, but printing and mid-air placing are **not** allowed.
* **Lighting:** Fullbright / Gamma modifications are allowed.
