If you are having trouble joining ALNSTCRAFT or linking your account, review the common solutions below before opening a support ticket.

## DiscordSRV & Account Linking Issues

### Bot Cannot DM You
If you execute the link command in-game and the Discord bot fails to send you a direct message, your Discord privacy settings are blocking direct messages from server members.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    #### Open Discord User Settings
    Click the **Gear icon** next to your profile in Discord.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    #### Navigate to Privacy & Safety
    Go to **Privacy & Safety** in the left sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    #### Enable Direct Messages
    Toggle **"Allow direct messages from server members"** to **ON** for the `.gg/vivinos` server.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    #### Retry Linking
    Re-run the linking command in-game to receive your verification code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connection & Access Issues

### Unable to Join After Linking (Role Missing)
If your account is linked but you receive a permission or whitelist error when attempting to join the Minecraft server, your Discord account is missing a required role.

To access ALNSTCRAFT, your Discord account must hold **at least one** of the following roles:
* `@VIVIs`
* `@MC Access`
* `@MC Tester`
* `@Moderators`

<Callout type="warn">
  If you believe you should have access, check your Discord profile roles in the `.gg/vivinos` server to confirm you hold at least one of these roles.
</Callout>

### Booster Perk / LuckPerms Sync Delays
If you recently boosted the Discord server or were assigned a new role but your permissions or perks have not updated in-game, there may be a temporary LuckPerms sync delay.

* **Re-log**: Disconnect and rejoin the server to force a permission update.
* **Wait for Sync**: Role synchronizations usually complete within a few minutes. If perks are still missing after 15 minutes, reach out in support.

## Getting Further Support

If your issue persists or isn't listed here, you can open a support ticket with our moderation team:

* **In-Server Support**: Head to the **`#support`** channel under the **Minecraft** category in our Discord server.
* **Main Server Support**: If you are banned from the Minecraft server or cannot view the Minecraft category, open a general support ticket in the main Discord server support channel.
